# 07 Reference: Kuehne+Nagel myKN portal

Reference only. Observed in the signed in myKN portal on 6 November 2026 by looking only; nothing was submitted. Do not copy their logo, colours, images or wording. Copy the layout ideas.

Screenshots (in `screenshots/`):
- `kuehne-nagel-01-quote-request-top.png`: the top of the Air quote request page.
- `kuehne-nagel-02-quote-request-totals-incoterms.png`: totals, chargeable weight and incoterms.

The screenshots show only the avatar initials of the signed in user.

## What was observed

- **Top bar:** dark blue. Logo and product name on the left, a centred search box "Search by tracking reference", a bell and a round avatar on the right.
- **Left sidebar:** thin icon rail that expands to labels, with a "Close sidebar" control at the bottom. Items: Home, Quote, Book, Track, Explore, News. Connectivity and Help at the bottom. The Quote item opens a flyout: All modes (New quote), Air (New quote), Sea (New quote and Tailored request), Road (New quote), My quotes.
- **Home:** quote cards for all modes, sea, air and road, each with "Quote now" and the text "Get a price in just two steps".
- **Quote request page (air):** four step header (1 Quote request, 2 Quote result, 3 Booking data, 4 Booking review) and a From / By / To summary row. Pick-up or Drop-off tabs and Delivery or To station tabs, each with an address dropdown. A packages area: "You can add packaging details individually or paste multiple at once", tabs Totals and Package details, a kg/cm and lb/inch switch, fields Total quantity, Total volume (m3), Total weight (kg) and an automatic Chargeable weight. Incoterms: radio Prepaid or Collect and a dropdown (DAP shown) with an explanation text ("Delivered At Place ... The seller bears all costs and risks until arrival at the designated destination, excluding discharge"). Buttons: "Get a quote" and "Save as template".

Not opened: the quote result, My quotes, booking steps, and the sea and road forms.

## What we take

- The dark top bar, user avatar menu, icon rail that expands.
- Mode cards on the entry page.
- The grouped form with a summary row (From / By / To).
- Totals and Package details tabs, unit switch, automatic chargeable weight.
- The incoterm explanation under the dropdown.
- Optional: "paste multiple at once" and "Save as template" (open items, default off).

## What we change

- No booking, tracking, explore or news. No four step booking flow.
- Our flow ends with "Request quotation", not an instant price. The customer sees no price until GAC sends the quotation.
- Our own neutral look with one accent colour.
- The customer has no sidebar at all; K+N users do.
